# Brand first-layer research record

Research date: 2026-09-03

## Method and interpretation

Each target was selected after reviewing the live result mix, manufacturer literature, the existing HVAC Bench query set, and question wording used in owner communities. No paid keyword-volume or keyword-difficulty dataset was available, so this record makes no numerical volume or KD claim. “Feasible” below is a qualitative editorial estimate based on observable result quality: exact-query coverage, model scoping, primary-source visibility, and whether ranking pages answered the diagnostic decision rather than merely repeating a code label.

Community discussions were used only to identify vocabulary and recurring decision confusion, such as whether a light is a fault, whether a reset is a repair, or whether the same display means the same thing across families. Technical claims and safety boundaries come from the OEM records named in each article’s `sourceIds` field.

## Cannibalization and publication decision

Existing HVAC Bench URLs were compared by brand, code, symptom, family, and intent. The selected targets either introduce a previously uncovered brand or answer a narrower model-specific decision than an existing generic troubleshooting page. Generic pages remain the upstream owner-symptom resources; the new brand pages own the exact brand/family query and link back to those generic procedures. No numerical search-demand claims were used to justify publication.

All 43 topics met the evidence threshold after model and region narrowing. None required community content as technical authority. Every published module carries at least two registered OEM/manufacturer evidence records, related article links, two glossary links, truthful `source-verified` review metadata, and structured decision content rendered by the existing article components.
